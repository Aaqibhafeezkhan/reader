💀 **Day of the Dead**\
Kept in Mexico on 1 and 2 November, when families gather to remember their dead with altars, marigolds and the foods they loved.

🕯️ **All Souls' Day**\
A Christian day of prayer and remembrance for the faithful departed, when many families visit and decorate their graves.

📰 **International Day to End Impunity for Crimes Against Journalists**\
A UN day, kept every 2 November, for the journalists killed for their work whose cases are never solved.

## Ways to embrace today

- For the Day of the Dead, read Wikipedia's [article on the day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_of_the_Dead), then tell someone a funny story about a person you miss.
- For All Souls' Day, find an old photo of a grandparent and write down one thing you know about their life.
- For journalists, read one piece of local reporting all the way through, and think about who'd tell that story if no one did.
  [Wikipedia's page on the day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Day_to_End_Impunity_for_Crimes_Against_Journalists) says why it's kept.

# [x] Singing Warmup

## Reset: Effort → Air

timer:[2m]

**Sighing "oo"**

- Start around pitch:[C4], slide down to pitch:[G3] and back up
- Vowel: "oo" as in "food"
- Very soft: warm air on glass
- No gripping, no effort

> [!TIP]
> If this feels trivial, you're doing it right.

## Falsetto Rehab

timer:[3m]

**Breathy "hoo"**

- Range: pitch:[G4] -> pitch:[C5]
- Light, airy onset: an owl sound
- Air leads, not cord closure
- No attempt to sound "good"

**Slightly cleaner "oo"**

- Same range (pitch:[G4] -> pitch:[C5])
- Reduce breathiness just a bit
- Still very light

> [!TIP]
> Goal: falsetto becomes something you can choose, not something that stops you cold.

## Mix Builder / Bridge

timer:[4m]

**"Gee" (like "geese")**

- 5-note scale: pitch:[E4] -> pitch:[A4], then move up each round
- Focus area: pitch:[G4] -> pitch:[B4]
- Forward placement: almost nasal, but not quite
- Light, speech-like: no weight added

> [!WARNING]
> If it starts to feel like pushing: reset lighter.

## Target Work: pitch:[A4] -> pitch:[C5]

timer:[5m]

**"Nay" (bratty tone)**

- Short bursts: pitch:[A4] -> pitch:[B4] -> pitch:[C5]
- Think: annoying cartoon voice, slight edge
- Accesses mix without adding weight

**Open vowel shaping**

- Sing "ah", then modify toward "uh" as pitch rises
- pitch:[B4]: not "AHHH", more like an "UH/AH blend"

**"Touch-and-go" pitch:[C5]**

- Tap it and back off: do not hold it
- Think: "visit, don't live there"

> [!WARNING]
> Resist the urge to prove you can hit pitch:[C5].
> That instinct reinforces the exact tension you're undoing.

Train it light, frequent, and boring.
The ceiling rises when you stop pushing at it.
