# 0.0.5 release review

Range: `v0.0.4..8c85976` on `review/2026.11.18` (174 commits, 228 files, about 10,500 lines added: main since v0.0.4, and this week's batch, #272, #219, #270, #263 and #208).
After the fixes below, and gh-302 and gh-305, which joined the batch during the review, 1387 tests and lint pass.

## How this review was done

Four reviewers, one per lens in [REVIEWING.md](../REVIEWING.md), each working read-only against 8c85976. The reviewers were Claude agents, each briefed with the same instructions; the maintainer chose where each finding went from their merged list, in the review's own file, before anything was fixed or filed.

- **Technical debt:** duplication, and whether the features stay apart (#141): no feature reaching into another.
- **Quality:** tests missing for what changed, found by breaking a line and seeing whether a test fails; lint, docs, CONTRIBUTING and the contributing skills.
- **Correctness:** bugs in what 0.0.5 is for, reading and annotation: cards under their labels, links to headings, items and cards, music, choices with blanks, dev mode, Work and Present.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA. axe-core 4.10.2, fetched for the review, as it isn't in the repo: its results at 8c85976 are the same as at v0.0.4, so every accessibility finding below is one axe can't see. Keyboard traces through choices with blanks, links within a page, a drawn tune and its player, and badged items; target sizes; the system's colours.

Each reviewer was given every open issue first, so as not to report what's known, and reproduced what it found in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, or by rendering. Each also wrote down what it looked at and decided was *not* a finding.

Each finding was then placed by REVIEWING.md's three questions:

1. **Is it new in this milestone, and small?** Then fix it before the tag, as a polish of the issue that brought it.
2. **Is it in the way of what 0.0.5 is for,** reading and annotation? Then 0.0.5.
3. **Otherwise,** the milestone whose theme it fits, or an issue already covering the same code.

And, as #81 adds, a finding for a feature added in this milestone goes in this milestone where it can.

No security findings.

## Found during the review

- **An aside carried on onto lines that weren't its own,** so every feature that reads lines ran with the asides in, and slipped on them: a `+1` after an aside at the margin under a card was left as typed, on v0.0.4 too. That became #305, which joined the batch: an aside is only its own `~` lines, taken out before anything else reads the page.
- **`browse.mjs` in Safari pressed only once on a page drawn again after a press,** though each step said ok; fixed in 9ac15a7. Safari checks before it with more than one press on a page may have missed this.

## Looked at and set aside

- **Deliberate:** #272's renaming of `style` before reading a page, safe as the renderer escapes every `<` in text; #208's page asking the server each second, only under `npm run dev`; the same patterns written once per runtime, server, app and frame, which can't share a module.
- **Covered elsewhere:** a box's name without its letter (#298), a task's date name (#268), the staff's size at 200% (#306), the note played (settled in #99's review), a ticked letter showing ✓ (#261's decision).
- **The same at v0.0.4, too small, or best practice only:** axe's banner and first-heading rules, progress bars named "Progress", a card label's name, the open blank's outline; Work's dots and the fold and card buttons pass 24 pixels by their spacing.
- **Checked and fine:** links to items and cards from far down a page; choices with blanks picked and counted by the keyboard, with parent boxes and sections; a pick that ticks a parent and finishes its section, all written back; Play, Pause and Back at 24 pixels, 7 to 1, and in the system's colours; browser marks named and shown in the system's colours; Present gone from a workbook; #219's cards with votes, asides and ids.
- **Unlikely:** a folder named `$reader` in someone's notes, which dev mode shadows; exporting one of reader's own files; two identical tunes on one page, sharing one player.

## The milestones that follow

This batch did seven issues and twelve fixes in about two days, but that isn't far enough ahead to pull work forward: 0.0.6's 28 open issues in the three and a half weeks to its date, 2026-12-13, are about this pace already. The later dates stay: 0.0.7 on 2027-01-10, 0.0.8 on 2027-01-23.

0.0.5 is tagged on its date, 2026-11-22. Its last three issues, #268, #296 and #298, all wait on PR #275; if it isn't back by then, they move to 0.0.6, to go in when it lands.

## What wasn't covered

- **More contrast:** `browse.mjs` has no option for it; the one more-contrast change in 0.0.5, cards' tint, is tested.
- **A real screen reader:** what's heard was inferred from the accessibility tree.
- **Windows** beyond CI, the desktop app, print and export.
