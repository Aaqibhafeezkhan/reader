Samples of what reader is for, each a folder of its own, to open in reader and make your own.

- [How to Take Notes Like a Boss](take-notes-like-a-boss/tutorial.md): a tutorial on a day of talks, from bullets typed fast to your own thoughts, questions, words to look up, quotes and things to do.
  It comes with the notes it practices on, as they start and as they finish.
- [Daily Journal](daily-journal/2026-11-02.md): a day of self-care as a workbook, a page at a time: exercise, meditation, to-dos, gratitude, reading, a singing warmup, and how you're feeling.
  It's someone's Monday, filled in and kept.

## Opening one

From a copy of reader's code, open this folder:

```console
$ reader samples/
```

Or, without the code, download a sample's folder from [the docs](https://jzheaux.github.io/reader/), unzip it into the folder reader shows, and it turns up in the file list.

## Making one your own

Copy the sample's folder into your own notes first, so what you change is yours and not reader's.

A dated sample, like the daily journal, starts your own day from it:
**Copy for today**, in the top bar, makes today's file beside it, with today's date and every answer cleared, and opens it, in Work if that's where you were.
Change what's in it to suit you, and the next morning **Start today's**, in the **Today** list, starts that day's from yours.
See [Files](https://jzheaux.github.io/reader/files.html#starting-todays) for how.

Any other sample is copied with **Make a copy**, which in Work starts it with its answers cleared.
